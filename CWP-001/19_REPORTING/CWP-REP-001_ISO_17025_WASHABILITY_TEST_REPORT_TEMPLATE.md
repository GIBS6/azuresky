## 1. Executive Summary & Regulatory Authority

Under **ISO/IEC 17025:2017 Clause 7.8** (*Reporting of results*) and **SANAS R 47** (*Rules for the Use of the SANAS Accreditation Symbol and Reference to Accreditation*), analytical test reports and certificates must present results clearly, accurately, unambiguously, and objectively.

AzureSky controls test certificate issuance through:
[`7.8. REPORTING OF RESULTS.docx`](file:///Z:/AZURESKY%20SYSTEM/AZURESKY%20QMS/17025/CLAUSE%207/7.8%20REPORTING/7.8.%20REPORTING%20OF%20RESULTS.docx) and [`AS010IW STATEMENTS OF CONFORMITY & DECISION RULES.docx`](file:///Z:/AZURESKY%20SYSTEM/AZURESKY%20QMS/PROCEDURES/AS010IW%20STATEMENTS%20OF%20CONFORMITY%20&%20DECISION%20RULES.docx).

This document, **`CWP-REP-001`**, establishes the standardized, auditable client-facing **Test Certificate and Washability Characterisation Report Template** for coal dense medium float-and-sink testing under **ISO 7936:2022**.

> [!NOTE]
> **SANAS R 47 ACCREDITATION STATUS STATEMENT**:
> Coal Washability Testing (ISO 7936:2022) and Particle Size Distribution (ISO 1953:2025) are currently **unaccredited analytical methods** at AzureSky (Pty) Ltd, operated under internal validation protocol [`CWP-001-VAL-001`](file:///c:/Projects/AzureSky/CWP-001/13_METHOD_VALIDATION/CWP-001-VAL-001_FLOAT_AND_SINK_METHOD_VALIDATION_DOSSIER.md) pending formal SANAS scope extension assessment. In accordance with SANAS R 47 Clause 4.3 and [`ASMF 5.3 REV 02`](file:///Z:/AZURESKY%20SYSTEM/AZURESKY%20QMS/17025/CLAUSE%205/5.3/ASMF%205.3%20REV%2002%20LABORATORY%20ACTIVITIES.docx), this certificate does **NOT** bear the SANAS accreditation logo, and washability results are clearly marked as unaccredited until SANAS accreditation is granted.
