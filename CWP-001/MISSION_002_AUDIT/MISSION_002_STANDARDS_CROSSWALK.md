## 1. Crosswalk Architecture & Evaluation Protocol

This standards crosswalk establishes the direct technical relationship between:
1. **Normative International & National Requirements** (verified against authentic editions).
2. **AzureSky's Live Operating QMS Procedures & Forms** (`Z:\AZURESKY SYSTEM\AZURESKY QMS\`).
3. **Available Objective Evidence** (actual observed practice, registers, and records).
4. **Demonstrated Method & Governance Gaps** (distinguishing missing records from technical deficiencies).
5. **Proposed Resolution & QMS Integration Actions** (under controlled change procedures).

### Conformity Status Key:
* **VERIFIED**: Requirement supported by direct objective evidence in the current controlled QMS.
* **PARTIALLY VERIFIED**: Technical foundation exists, but an essential operational or evidence element is incomplete.
* **UNVERIFIED**: Insufficient objective evidence to confirm compliance or execution.
* **GAP IDENTIFIED**: Required control or technical element is demonstrably absent or contrary to standard.
