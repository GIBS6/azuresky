## 1. Document Control Taxonomy & Classification

In accordance with the CWP-001 Document Control Protocol, every document referenced across the project must be assigned an auditable classification status with an exact, clickable `file:///` link:

* 🟢 **CURRENT CONTROLLED DOCUMENT**: An approved, active, and legally binding procedure, policy, form, or record within AzureSky's live operating QMS.
* 🟡 **REFERENCE / EXTERNAL DOCUMENT**: An external standard (e.g., ISO, SANS, ASTM), statutory regulation (OHSA, NEMWA), or external calibration certificate issued by an accredited third-party provider (Micron Lab, Montech).
* 🔵 **CWP-001 WORKING DOCUMENT**: An engineering study, verification matrix, technical specification, or design artifact generated within the CWP-001 workspace repository.
* 🔴 **OBSOLETE — HISTORICAL ONLY**: A superseded revision or archived procedure retained strictly for historical evolution and change-tracking; **must not be cited as current operating authority**.
* ⚪ **REQUIRED EXTENSION / TO BE DEVELOPED**: A missing document, procedure, validation dossier, or form identified as necessary to bridge the gap to full SANAS accreditation.
