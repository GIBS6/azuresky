### SECTION B: TECHNICAL PRINCIPLES MEMO (30 MARKS)

**Question B1 (8 Marks):**
- *Stokes' Law Explanation (4 marks)*: Terminal settling velocity is proportional to particle diameter squared ($v \propto d^2$) and inversely proportional to medium viscosity ($\mu$). In concentrated $\text{ZnCl}_2$ ($\mu \approx 3.0\text{ to }15.0\text{ mPa}\cdot\text{s}$), fine particles $< 0.50\text{ mm}$ have near-zero terminal velocity ($v < 0.1\text{ mm/s}$), meaning they remain suspended indefinitely and cannot achieve static equilibrium.
- *Handling Protocol (4 marks)*: Raw sample is screened at $0.50\text{ mm}$ prior to washing. The $-0.50\text{ mm}$ fines are weighed, dried at $105^\circ\text{C}$, analyzed directly for raw Proximate/CV quality, and mathematically accounted for in the composite balance without density separation.

**Question B2 (6 Marks):**
- *Protocol (3 marks)*: Submerge coarse coal charge in clean water for **2 minutes**, then drain on a $0.5\text{ mm}$ screen for **5 minutes**.
- *Physical Purpose (3 marks)*: Fills open internal pores with water, creating a surface-moist, pore-saturated state. This prevents capillary absorption of high-density $\text{ZnCl}_2$ liquid into the coal during testing, eliminating false sink misplacement.

**Question B3 (8 Marks):**
- *Procedure (4 marks)*: Agitate bath to prevent vertical stratification; fill clean $500\text{ mL}$ cylinder; measure temperature with calibrated RTD thermometer; gently insert clean Class M50 hydrometer; allow to settle free of cylinder walls.
- *Reading & Correction (4 marks)*: Read bottom of meniscus at eye level (avoiding parallax). Apply formula: $\text{RD}_{20} = \text{RD}_{\text{meas}} + 0.00075 \times (T_{\text{meas}} - 20.0)$. Acceptance criterion: $|\text{RD}_{20} - \text{RD}_{\text{target}}| \le 0.002\text{ g/cm}^3$.

**Question B4 (8 Marks):**
- *Invalidity of Visual Cleanliness (4 marks)*: $\text{ZnCl}_2$ is a completely clear, colourless salt solution. Coal rinsed in contaminated water appears visually identical to coal rinsed in pure water. Visual inspection provides zero metrological evidence of residual salt under ISO/IEC 17025 Clause 7.7.
- *EC and AgNO₃ Verification (4 marks)*: Electrical conductivity ($\text{EC}$) quantitatively measures dissolved ionic species. Final run-off must be $< 100\text{ }\mu\text{S/cm}$ (or background $+30\text{ }\mu\text{S/cm}$). $\text{AgNO}_3$ spot test precipitates white $\text{AgCl}$ in the presence of trace chloride ions; a clear drop confirms complete chloride removal.

In a commercial washery, this indicates a high risk of misplaced material, requiring precision dense medium cyclone control, high medium stability, and tight density monitoring.
