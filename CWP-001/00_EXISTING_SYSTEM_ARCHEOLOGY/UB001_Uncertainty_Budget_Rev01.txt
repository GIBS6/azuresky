MASTER MEASUREMENT UNCERTAINTY BUDGET
(ISO/IEC 17025:2017 Clause 7.6 & SANAS F49 Requirements)
1. Purpose and Scope
This consolidated uncertainty budget encompasses all calibrated instruments used in the analytical determination of moisture, ash, volatile matter, fixed carbon, and calorific value.All calibrations are traceable to NMISA through SANAS-accredited laboratories (Micron Lab & Montech Calibration Services).
2. The Crux / Nucleus of Method Validation
Performance characteristics (range, accuracy, precision, repeatability) are clearly established.
Methods and instruments are validated and verified for representative coal sample types.
Results meet customer and method specifications, ensuring traceable, reliable measurements.

6. Combined Analytical Uncertainty
Propagation of the individual equipment uncertainties shows that total analytical uncertainty remains well below the precision limits of ISO methods 589, 1171, 562, 5925 and ISO 1928.Therefore, measurement uncertainty does not materially affect analytical results, confirming fitness for purpose.
7. Traceability and Compliance
All instruments are traceable to national standards through SANAS-accredited calibration.Each certificate explicitly references ISO/IEC 17025 compliance, ensuring an unbroken chain of traceability to the SI units.
